Common Questions

Quick answers to what most customers want to know.

How often should I have my septic tank pumped? ▾
For most households, every 3–5 years is the standard recommendation. Actual frequency depends on tank size and the number of occupants. We can assess your specific system and advise accordingly.
What are the signs my septic tank needs service? ▾
Slow drains, gurgling sounds, sewage odors inside or outside, wet spots in the yard above the drain field, or sewage backup in fixtures are all warning signs. If you notice any of these, call us promptly.
Do you offer same-day service? ▾
Yes. We're open 7 days a week and often accommodate same-day calls. Call (614) 837-3922 and we'll get a team out to you as quickly as possible.
How much does septic pumping cost? ▾
Pricing depends on tank size, access, and location. We provide transparent, upfront quotes before any work begins — no surprises. Contact us for a free estimate.
Are you licensed and insured to work in Obetz and the greater Columbus area? ▾
Absolutely. Ace Septic is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the state of Ohio. We operate in full compliance with all local and state regulations.
